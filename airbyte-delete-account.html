AirByte Wallet & Data Deletion (데이터 및 지갑 삭제 안내)

AirByte does not require personal registration (email/password). It uses an anonymous Device Wallet ID. You can permanently delete all server records using either method below:

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (앱 내부에서 1초 즉시 삭제 - 권장)

  1. Open the AirByte app. (AirByte 앱 실행)
  2. Open the top-left Side Menu (≡). (좌측 상단 메뉴 클릭)
  3. Tap Delete Wallet Data and confirm Delete Now. (Delete Wallet Data 선택 후 즉시 파기)

Result: Your Device UUID, remaining quota balance, and gift PIN logs are immediately and permanently erased from our Supabase database.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (웹/이메일 삭제 요청)

If you have already uninstalled the app, send your 8-character Wallet ID (or full Device UUID) to our Data Protection Officer. All records will be purged within 24 hours.
