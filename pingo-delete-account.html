Pingo (com.jgsoft.pingolive)

Account & Data Deletion Request
계정 및 개인정보 영구 삭제 안내

JG Soft (제이지소프트) provides users of the Pingo app with two immediate ways to permanently delete their account, Circle profile, location history, and all associated personal data.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (앱 내부에서 즉시 삭제)

  1. Open the Pingo app on your smartphone. (Pingo 앱을 실행합니다.)
  2. Tap the Settings / Profile (설정 / 프로필) icon at the top or bottom sheet.
  3. Select "Delete Account & Data (계정 및 데이터 영구 삭제)" and confirm.
  4. Your profile, real-time location coordinates, Safe Places, and voice ping logs are immediately and permanently deleted from our Supabase servers.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (앱 삭제 후 웹에서 삭제 요청)

If you have already uninstalled the app, enter your Circle PIN or Nickname below to submit a direct deletion request to our Data Protection Officer (processed within 24 hours).
이미 앱을 삭제하신 경우 아래 양식을 통해 삭제 요청을 보내주시면 영업일 기준 24시간 이내에 서버에서 모든 데이터를 영구 파기하고 회신해 드립니다.

Data Deleted vs. Retained (삭제되는 데이터 및 보관 기간)

  • Immediately Deleted (즉시 영구 파기 항목): User nickname, Circle membership, GPS location coordinates, Safe Place geofences, temporary Safe-Return web tokens, and 3-second PTT audio clips.
  • Retained by Law (법령에 따른 예외 보관): In-app purchase transaction receipts are managed directly by Apple App Store / Google Play under applicable tax and e-commerce laws.
